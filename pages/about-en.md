# About Me

I'm Tiago Zaniquelli, an **AppSec Specialist** with over 20 years of experience in the Information Technology field. My career is built on a solid foundation of 18 years as a developer and software architect before fully transitioning into defensive security in 2021.

## My Philosophy

I don't believe in miracle tools. I believe that high-level application security stems from a robust **security culture**, well-defined processes, and, above all, the empowerment of people.

My work focuses on building and evolving security programs (using frameworks like **OWASP SAMM**), implementing **DevSecOps** pipelines, and, fundamentally, spreading security awareness through **Security Champions** programs.

## Experience and Community

Currently, I work in Information Security Architecture, tackling challenges ranging from AI architecture security to establishing secure development standards at scale.

I am an active member of the security community, frequently speaking at conferences such as **BSides SP**, **Mind The Sec**, **Roadsec**, and **TDC**. I believe that sharing knowledge is the best way to raise the security bar for everyone.
